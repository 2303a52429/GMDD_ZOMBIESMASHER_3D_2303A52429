Featured project

Zombie Smasher 3D 🧟

Third-person, three-lane zombie endless runner · Unity 6 (URP) · C#

Run through four post-apocalyptic worlds, dodge obstacles, outrun five kinds of zombies and grab power-ups while a pursuing horde chases you down. Built with a four-person team as a Game Mechanics course project at SR University.

  • 4 themed worlds: Dense Zombie Forest, Desert, Spooky Dark City and Abandoned City, each with its own lighting, fog, post-processing and music
  • 5 zombie types (walkers, runners, brutes, hordes, emergers) and a pursuing horde that catches you on a second stumble
  • 5 power-ups: Magnet, Speed Boost, Shield, Double Coins and Health Protection
  • Rising difficulty from 11 to 25 m/s, with zombie density and obstacle patterns scaling with distance
  • Fully procedural content: every model, texture, sound effect and music track is generated in code
  • Performance: object pooling, chunk streaming, static mesh merging, floating origin and quality tiers
Unity 6000.6URPC#Input SystemWindowsAndroid

🏆 Best run: 2,654 points over 845 m in the Forest theme
